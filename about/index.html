XU Yujie(b. 1995, Guilin, China)
Animation director, illustrator and visual artist now based in Bloomington.
Her work explores the ambiguity and authenticity within memory, emotion, and human relations, responding in flowing images to how technology, language, and space subtly influence the individual's perception of reality. Her films have been shown in different international film festivals such as Slamdance Film Festival, Ottawa International Animation Festival, Video Art Miden, Tricky Women/Tricky Realities Festival, CURRENTS 2024 Art & Technology Festival, and many more.


徐雨洁，1995年出生于中国桂林。
独立动画导演，插画师，视觉艺术家，现工作和生活于布鲁明顿。
她的创作探索记忆、情感和人际联结中的模糊与真实，在流动的影像中回应技术、语言与空间如何潜移默化地影响个体对现实的感知。曾入围过国际上多个大型影展诸如美国Slamdance电影节、加拿大渥太华国际动画节、希腊Video Art Miden影展、奥地利Tricky Women影展、美国CURRENTS艺术与科技展览等等。
