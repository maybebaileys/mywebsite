XU Yujie(b. 1995, Guilin, China)
Animation director, illustrator and visual artist, currently works and lives in Bloomington, IN.
Her work explores the ambiguity and authenticity within memory, emotion, and human relations, responding in flowing images to how technology, language, and space subtly influence the individual's perception of reality. Her films have been shown in different international film festivals such as Slamdance Film Festival, Ottawa International Animation Festival, Video Art Miden, Tricky Women/Tricky Realities Festival, CURRENTS Art & Technology Festival, and many more.


徐雨潔，1995年出生於中國桂林。
独立獨立動畫導演，插畫家，視覺藝術家，現工作和生活於印第安納布魯明頓。
她的創作探索記憶、情感和人際連結中的模糊與真實，在流動的影像中回應技術、語言與空間如何潛移默化地影響個體對現實的感知。曾入圍國際上多個大型影展諸如美國Slamdance電影節、加拿大渥太華國際動畫節、希臘Video Art Miden影展、奧地利Tricky Women影展、美國CURRENTS藝術與科技展等等。
